Graph definition“The Cayley graph on binary words with k zeros”Schreier construction: words represent cosets, not all permutations.
Coset definition“The Schreier graph associated with Sn/(Sk × Sn−k)”Correct name and subgroup.

Precision: “not a Cayley graph” refers to this construction on cosets. Some Schreier graphs are also isomorphic to Cayley graphs of another group; a non-regular example cannot be. Here the quotient is a coset space, not generally a quotient group.

The bead analogy

Imagine one room for every arrangement of n labeled beads. Repaint labels 0 through k−1 dark and all remaining labels light. You can no longer distinguish beads of the same color. Blocks of k!(n−k)! rooms become one room.

Why the names differ

Cayley vertices are group elements; Schreier vertices are left cosets σH, where H = Stab(A) = Sₖ × Sₙ₋ₖ and A = 0ᵏ1ⁿ⁻ᵏ. The projection π(σ) = σ·A is one-to-one only when H is trivial. Abstract graph shapes can coincide in other cases.

Moves and connectivity

A consecutive c-cycle rotates its window left by one place; its inverse rotates right. We use left multiplication, (g∘σ)[i] = g[σ[i]], so π(g∘σ) = g·π(σ). Cayley graphs are always vertex-transitive and regular, and connected exactly when the generators generate the group. Schreier graphs need be neither connected nor vertex-transitive; their degrees can vary after loops and duplicate edges are removed.

What the collapse costs

Rooms merge, distinct edges merge, and moves within one fiber disappear as loops. Thus a distance lower bound read from the coarse graph can be strictly below the original distance. The percentage shown counts edges that become loops, not all merged parallel edges.

Using the explorer

Hover either graph to highlight one fiber on both sides. START collapses the left graph in 2.6 seconds; RESET restores its deterministic layout. At n = 6, the 720 original vertices use dots colored by fiber for readability, then resolve into two-color bead rows. If c > n, there is no legal window and both graphs have no edges.
